The approach

We do not start with “how do we stop this behaviour?”

We start with understanding. What is the student experiencing? What is happening underneath the presentation? What language, confidence or self-awareness might be missing?

EmpowerED Minds school programmes are designed to sit alongside existing pastoral, SEND and wellbeing provision.
