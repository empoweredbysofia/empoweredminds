Your 12 month journey

A new theme each month

Explore your emotions, values, needs and relationships, and build confidence in the choices you make. Each session stands on its own, while the themes build on one another over the year.

Explore the 12 monthly themes
  1. 01

    How am I, really?

    Recognising your emotions and understanding what you might need.

  2. 02

    When everything feels too much

    Understanding your emotional capacity and what supports or drains you.

  3. 03

    My thoughts are not always facts

    Separating what happened from what you believe it means.

  4. 04

    What matters to me?

    Exploring your values alongside responsibilities and expectations.

  5. 05

    What is mine to carry?

    Recognising your responsibilities and what is outside your control.

  6. 06

    Can I choose myself without feeling guilty?

    Making room for rest, support and things that matter to you.

  7. 07

    My needs deserve a voice

    Recognising your needs and practising how to express them.

  8. 08

    Boundaries in everyday life

    Communicating what you can offer and where your limits are.

  9. 09

    When people see things differently

    Navigating different values and expectations in relationships.

  10. 10

    How do I speak to myself?

    Responding to self-criticism with a fairer, more supportive voice.

  11. 11

    Trusting myself again

    Building confidence through small choices and recognising strengths.

  12. 12

    What do I want to carry forward?

    Reflecting on your journey and choosing your next small step.

A few things you might want to know

Do I have to talk or share personal details?

No. You can speak, use the chat, listen or quietly reflect. You choose how much you share.

How often does the Circle meet?

Once a month, on the first Friday at 11am UK time. Each online session lasts 60 minutes, with regular group check-ins between sessions.

What happens after I request the free session?

Complete the registration form on this page. Sofia will email you to confirm your place and share the Google Meet joining details. Please check your junk folder too.

How do I join the paid Circle?

On Substack, select Individual and choose Monthly at £8 a month, or Annual at £80 a year, paid upfront. The yearly option saves £16 compared with 12 monthly payments. Both paid options include the monthly Circle.

Are there still in-person meetups?

Yes. We will continue in-person events and meetups. Enquire about the local group for updates. Sofia verifies your identity before adding you.
