The purpose

To help you understand the way your mind works, make sense of your experiences and begin working with yourself instead of constantly fighting yourself.

Who it is for

Maybe you finally have a name for what you have been carrying.

You do not need to arrive with everything figured out. Late2ADHD™ creates space to understand your patterns without reducing you to a list of symptoms.

  • ✓You received an ADHD diagnosis later in life
  • ✓You are waiting for an assessment
  • ✓You strongly recognise ADHD traits in yourself
  • ✓You have often felt lazy, inconsistent, emotional or ‘too much’
  • ✓You want understanding and practical support without shame

The six-week journey

One meaningful focus at a time.

Each session combines one clear teaching point, guided reflection, shared discussion and one practical change to explore. Spacious, supportive and designed for an ADHD mind.

01

The Brain You Have Always Had

Look back through a new lens, make sense of long-standing patterns and begin separating your identity from years of self-blame.

02

Why Everyday Life Can Feel So Hard

Understand executive functioning, task initiation, time, memory, transitions and the invisible effort behind ordinary days.

03

Your Emotions Are Not Too Much

Explore emotional intensity, sensitivity, frustration, rejection and what happens when your nervous system reaches capacity.

04

The Cost of Trying to Look Fine

Notice masking, overcompensating, people-pleasing, perfectionism and the patterns that can quietly lead to burnout.

05

Working With Yourself

Create realistic systems, supportive environments, clearer communication and boundaries that fit the way you actually function.

06

Acceptance Without Giving Up

Bring your learning together and create a personal ADHD roadmap rooted in self-knowledge, compassion and practical support.

What makes it different

01

Understanding before change

We begin with curiosity, context and the reason behind the pattern.

02

Self-acceptance is the starting point for support, not the end of growth.

03
