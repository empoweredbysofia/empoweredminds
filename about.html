About Sofia Iqbal

Understanding people changes what becomes possible.

Sofia Iqbal is a scientist turned entrepreneur and Emotional Capacity Educator creating programmes, talks and learning experiences across individuals, schools and communities.

The approach

Science, entrepreneurship, lived experience and understanding first.

Sofia’s work brings together a scientific way of thinking, psychology-informed learning, emotional change work, entrepreneurship, neurodiversity awareness and real-world delivery.

EmpowerED Minds exists because understanding changes the questions we ask. Instead of reducing people to behaviour, labels or what they are struggling with, the work looks at what may be happening underneath and what becomes possible when capacity increases.
